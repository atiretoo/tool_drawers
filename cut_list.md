# Workbench Drawer Subsystem Cut List & BOM

This list provides the exact dimensions for building the carcass, the 5-drawer graduated box stack, and the false fronts. 

## Bill of Materials (BOM)

### Lumber & Sheet Goods
*   **3/4" (19.05 mm) Plywood**: For the carcass box (top, bottom, and vertical dividers). Requires approx. 1 full 4x8 sheet.
*   **1/2" (12.7 mm) Plywood**: For the drawer boxes (sides, fronts/backs, flush bottoms) and the false fronts. Baltic birch or cabinet-grade recommended. Requires approx. 1 full 4x8 sheet.

### Hardware
*   **Drawer Slides**: 5 pairs of **28" (711.2 mm)** full-extension side-mount ball-bearing slides (e.g., Accuride 3832 series or similar 100lb rated slides). Standard thickness: 1/2" (12.7 mm).
*   **Fasteners**: 
    *   1-1/4" or 1-1/2" pocket hole screws or trim-head cabinet screws for carcass assembly.
    *   1" trim-head screws for drawer box assembly (if butt joining).
    *   5/8" pan-head screws for slide mounting.
*   **Adhesive**: High-quality wood glue (e.g., Titebond II/III).
